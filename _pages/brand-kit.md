## Usage Rules

- Use **Command Black** and **Navy Grid** as base layers for most screens.
- Use **Signal Cyan** for primary actions, active states, and high-priority data.
- Use **Intel Violet** for secondary actions and deep-tech highlights.
- Keep body text in high contrast (`#EAF2FF` or equivalent) over dark backgrounds.
- Avoid warm, playful palettes for core product/brand surfaces.

## Typography Direction

- **Headlines:** bold, compact sans-serif (tech-forward, high legibility).
- **Body:** neutral sans-serif with comfortable line-height for analyst readability.
- **Numerical data:** monospaced or tabular forms where precision is required.
